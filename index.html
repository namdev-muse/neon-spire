NEON SPIRE

Latch the rings. Pump the swing. Release at the top of the arc — and fly.

3D graphics couldn't start in this browser — Neon Spire needs WebGL. Try a recent Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a device with graphics support.

How to Play

  • You hang from a grapple rope on the face of a neon spire. There is no climbing up by hand — the swing is the way up.
  • Hold Space / mouse / GRAPPLE to latch the nearest anchor ring in reach. The rope only reaches the rings you can actually fly to — no auto-aim.
  • Pump with A / D or Left / Right in time with the swing — push with the motion, not against it — to build amplitude.
  • Release Space at the top of the arc, while you are flying up toward the next ring, and latch it mid-flight. Release too early and you fall back; too late and the swing carries you past.
  • Collect gold orbs along the good lines for score. Touch a checkpoint ring and a fall sends you back there, not to the base.
  • Fall below your last checkpoint and you drop back to it. 5 falls ends the climb. Latch the gold summit ring to clear the climb.
  • Your best score is saved per climb — beat it. Clearing a climb unlocks the next one.

Controls

  • Hold Space / mouse button: grapple · A / D or Left / Right: pump the swing · Release Space: let go
  • P: pause · M: mute · R: restart
  • Touch: hold GRAPPLE, tap ◀ / ▶ to pump
  • Esc closes a panel · Enter confirms on menus
